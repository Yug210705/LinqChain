## 2. Automatic Renewal Terms (Continuous Service)
- Subscriptions renew automatically at the conclusion of each billing term (monthly or annual).
- LinqChain dispatches an advance reminder notification at least 30 calendar days prior to annual renewal dates detailing upcoming fees.
- Customer payment methods (corporate credit card or ACH direct debit) are charged automatically on the first day of each renewed billing period.

---

## 3. Cancellation & Termination Rights
- **Self-Service Cancellation:** Customers may disable auto-renewal directly in the platform via **Settings → Organization & Subscription** or by emailing `billing@linqchain.com`.
- **Notice Period:** Notice of non-renewal must be provided at least 30 days prior to the expiration of the current subscription term.
- **Access Continuation:** Cancellation takes effect at the conclusion of the paid billing cycle; access to platform data and export tools remains active until the expiration date.

---

## 4. Refund Policy & 14-Day Statutory Cooling-Off
1. **Statutory 14-Day Right:** Eligible entities in the European Union, United Kingdom, and jurisdictions with statutory cooling-off protections may request a full refund within 14 days of initial subscription activation without stating reasons.
2. **General Enterprise Policy:** Beyond statutory cooling-off periods, enterprise subscription fees are non-refundable except where LinqChain breaches its 99.9% SLA commitment.
